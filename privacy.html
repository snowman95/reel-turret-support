About this app

The current iOS version of Reel Turret is a paid, single-player game by Auto. It does not require an account and has no chat or user-generated content. Your progress, unlocks, a run in progress, language, volume and tip settings are stored only on your device. The game does not send these records to a game server. Your device backup service may retain a copy.

Current iOS version: no advertising or tracking

The paid iOS release (version 1.0.0, build 10 and later) contains no advertising, Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, or advertising tracking. We do not collect or transmit personal information, identifiers, usage analytics, or gameplay records from this iOS app. Buying the app is handled by Apple; we do not receive your payment-card details. There are no in-app purchases.

Other platforms and older test builds

Earlier ad-supported iOS TestFlight builds and the separate Android test version may contain Google Mobile Ads and consent features. Those builds may allow Google and its partners to process IP address, approximate location, device/advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for ads. See Google's partner-app data policy. This advertising description does not apply to the current paid iOS release.

The Steam edition uses Valve's platform services for features such as achievements. Steam handles its account and achievement information under the Steam Privacy Policy. These Steam features are not included in the iOS app.

Children

Reel Turret is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Requests and updates

If you contact us by email, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. For questions or requests about this app's data, contact snowman95tube@gmail.com. You can erase local progress with Settings → Reset progress, or by deleting the app (subject to your device backup settings). We will post policy changes on this page with an updated effective date.
